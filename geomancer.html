Geomancer

Give it any terrain, and it gives you back a world that looks real.

A worldbuilding toolkit for Unreal Engine 5, starting with water that actually understands the landscape underneath it. Oceans, lakes and rivers placed from the terrain's own shape — no splines to draw, no basins to hunt for by hand.

What it does today

Working now, in the current build

Everything below is in the build as it stands. None of it is roadmap.

  • One click, whatever the landscape

    Drop the placer into the level and hit Generate. Ocean, lakes and rivers come out placed from the terrain's own shape — no splines, no manual basin hunting.

  • Rivers follow the real drainage

    They rise where water would actually gather, merge where valleys meet, and every one of them ends somewhere real: the sea, a lake, or another river. No dead ends in open ground.

  • Closed basins hold water

    An inland depression with no outlet becomes an inland sea or a wetland, not a dry pit with rivers vanishing into it. Shallow pans that rivers feed stay wet.

  • It arrives dressed

    Shore materials, wave behaviour, buoyancy-ready water bodies — real engine water, not a visual shell.

  • It holds up at any distance

    Fly from the shoreline to the stratosphere and the world stays whole: near detail up close, simplified detail far away, nothing missing.

  • It is repeatable

    Generate twice on the same terrain and you get the same world back, down to the log output.
